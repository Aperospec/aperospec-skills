## Validation Checklist

Before finalizing, confirm:
- Input was normalized to one `TOPIC`.
- Output is a CWP, not an essay.
- No slide, page, layout, typography, or visual-design language appears.
- No film script or narrative world has been generated yet.
- Cognitive triggers are perceptible states, not abstract concepts.
- Narrative potential exists, but narrative execution is left to `aperospec-cinema`.
- The CWP can be passed directly into `aperospec-cinema`.

## Forbidden Drift

Do not drift into:
- PPT outline
- slide deck
- scene design
- screenplay
- visual world
- business plan
- module structure
- execution proposal

When uncertain, stay upstream:
- cause before story
- drive force before conflict scene
- perceptible trigger before image design
- future projection before worldbuilding
