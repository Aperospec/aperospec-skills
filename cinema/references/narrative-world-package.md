# Narrative World Package V2 API Template

Use this template only when the input is a complete Cognitive World Package (CWP) from `project` and that CWP is grounded in Aperospec V2 Stage 1 plus a user-selected focus.

## Input Gate

Before translation, confirm the CWP contains:

- ACTIVE FRAME AND LIMITS
- MATERIAL CONTRADICTIONS
- TEMPORAL FORMATION
- POSITIONS AND OPERATING FORCE FIELD
- BASELINE FUTURE JUDGMENT
- SELECTED FOCUS
- HUMAN CONSEQUENCES AND NARRATIVE PRESSURE
- PERCEPTIBLE IMPLICATIONS
- NARRATIVE POTENTIAL
- REVISION SIGNALS

If these fields are missing, do not improvise. Return to `project` for a valid CWP, or to Aperospec Stage 1 when the upstream reality model itself is incomplete.

## Mapping Table

| CWP field | NWP translation |
| --- | --- |
| ACTIVE FRAME AND LIMITS | WORLD boundaries and the surface framing the narrative must not reproduce uncritically |
| MATERIAL CONTRADICTIONS | unresolved pressure inside EMOTIONAL ENVIRONMENT and CORE CONFLICT |
| TEMPORAL FORMATION | WORLD history, momentum, path dependence, and atmosphere |
| POSITIONS AND OPERATING FORCE FIELD | invisible forces, dependencies, actor behavior, and pressure relationships |
| BASELINE FUTURE JUDGMENT | SOMETHING IS COMING |
| SELECTED FOCUS | the narrative center and governing CORE CONFLICT |
| HUMAN CONSEQUENCES AND NARRATIVE PRESSURE | EMOTIONAL CURVE and IMMERSIVE EXPERIENCE |
| PERCEPTIBLE IMPLICATIONS | SCENE POTENTIALS |
| NARRATIVE POTENTIAL | visual and narrative direction across the NWP |
| REVISION SIGNALS | uncertainty boundaries and conditions that forbid false narrative closure |

The mapping is a translation, not a new reality analysis. Preserve the user's selected focus exactly; Cinema may dramatize its pressure but may not replace or reprioritize it.

## Validation Checklist

Before finalizing, confirm:

- The input was a valid Aperospec V2-aligned CWP.
- The user's selected focus remains the narrative center.
- No new Stage 1 reality analysis, focus ranking, Stage 2 interest function, or strategy was invented.
- The baseline future judgment was translated rather than replaced by unsupported scenarios.
- Material contradictions, evidence limits, and revision signals remain visible.
- No slide titles, page structures, typography, layout, or motion design appear.
- Scene potentials are cinematic but not yet concept-deck beats.
- The NWP can be passed directly to `conceptdeck`.

## Forbidden Drift

Do not drift into:

- reopening or simplifying the upstream reality model without evidence
- replacing the user-selected focus with a more dramatic one
- treating public positions as complete causes
- inventing secret motives for narrative effect
- false closure that erases contradiction or uncertainty
- slide deck outline, concept deck, visual design instructions, business structure, or PPT language

When uncertain, map:

- ACTIVE FRAME AND LIMITS to world boundaries
- MATERIAL CONTRADICTIONS to unresolved pressure
- TEMPORAL FORMATION to world history and momentum
- POSITIONS AND OPERATING FORCE FIELD to actor relationships and invisible forces
- BASELINE FUTURE JUDGMENT to approaching change
- SELECTED FOCUS to narrative center and conflict
- HUMAN CONSEQUENCES AND NARRATIVE PRESSURE to emotional transition
- PERCEPTIBLE IMPLICATIONS to scene potentials
- NARRATIVE POTENTIAL to tone and pacing
- REVISION SIGNALS to open uncertainty and correction conditions
