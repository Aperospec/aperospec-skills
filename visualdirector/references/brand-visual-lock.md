# Brand Visual Lock

Use a Brand Visual Lock only when a project, account, product family, or recurring series needs durable visual recognition across outputs.

It is a small set of approved relationships, not a fixed template and not a substitute for Art Direction.

## Ownership and State

Runtime manages the state: `Pending -> Draft -> Approved -> Locked`.

VisualDirector may propose or apply a lock. It may not silently change a locked rule. A new or materially changed lock requires human approval because it changes durable visual identity.

If no Brand Visual Lock exists, VisualDirector can complete the current task without inventing one. Create one only when repetition across outputs creates real value.

## What May Be Locked

- type personality and practical weight range;
- color relationships rather than a universal fixed palette;
- margin, grid, source, caption, and account-identifier grammar;
- image, screenshot, evidence, and diagram treatment;
- recurring material, light, edge, or contrast behavior;
- explicit forbidden shortcuts.

## What Must Stay Flexible

- exact composition and subject placement;
- number and role of pages;
- information density;
- camera, crop, visual metaphor, and evidence role;
- whether a page is image-led, evidence-led, diagram-led, type-led, or quiet;
- channel-specific hierarchy.

If too many decisions are locked, the system has become a template. Reduce the lock until new content can still produce an original Art Direction.

## Application Rules

For each VDP, record:

- rules applied;
- rules intentionally unused because they conflict with the page task;
- any conflict with CDP, Style DNA, rights, platform, or legibility;
- whether the conflict can be resolved locally or needs a Runtime / human decision.

Content, evidence, rights, accessibility, and legibility outrank visual consistency. A locked identity rule may be revised only through the human Brand Direction Gate, never silently ignored.
