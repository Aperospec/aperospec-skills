# Aperospec Cinematic Deck Patterns

## Five-Act Deck Template

```markdown
## ACT 1: Opening World
Purpose:
[What world is being opened?]

Slides:
- Establishing Shot: [world-scale image + huge title]
- Wide Shot: [civilization / city / history / atmosphere]

## ACT 2: Emotional Conflict
Purpose:
[What rupture, loneliness, desire, or contradiction appears?]

Slides:
- Silent Shot: [black frame / single line / emotional pause]
- Documentary Shot: [human behavior or historical evidence]

## ACT 3: Emerging Future
Purpose:
[What future begins to appear?]

Slides:
- Transition Shot: [old world to new world]
- Wide Shot: [future civilization / digital space / virtual-real fusion]

## ACT 4: System Reveal
Purpose:
[What system, product, structure, UI, data, or business logic is revealed?]

Slides:
- Insert Shot: [credible detail / data / interface]
- System Shot: [cinematic technology system, not consulting diagram]

## ACT 5: Civilization Vision
Purpose:
[What future aftertaste remains?]

Slides:
- Wide Shot: [new era / civilization vision]
- Silent Shot: [final belief / emotional echo]
```

## Slide Card Template

```markdown
Slide title:
[Title as visual object, not explanation.]

Act:
[Opening World / Emotional Conflict / Emerging Future / System Reveal / Civilization Vision]

Shot type:
[Establishing Shot / Wide Shot / Medium Shot / Close-up / Insert Shot / Transition Shot / Silent Shot]

Emotional target:
[curiosity / immersion / civilization / history / loneliness / future / imagination / belief]

Information density:
[silent / low / medium / system-only]

Visual direction:
[cinematic scene, space, light, time, human traces, atmosphere]

Narration:
[film narration, civilization documentary, future manifesto, or exhibition introduction tone]
```

## Image Prompt Template

```text
cinematic, documentary, atmospheric, immersive,
emotional architecture, future civilization,
digital heritage, urban memory, spatial storytelling,
future archaeology, human traces, brutal contrast,
poetic technology,
[specific world / city / space / civilization / human trace],
[light condition],
[emotional temperature],
no generic tech image, no startup style, no e-commerce look
```

## Rhythm Check

Before finalizing a deck, check:
- Does it begin with a world rather than a product?
- Are data and UI delayed until ACT 4?
- Does every slide have one visual core?
- Are there black frames or silent pages for breathing?
- Does the ending feel like a new era rather than a company summary?
- Does the deck move like a film rather than a report?

## Forbidden Drift

Do not drift into:
- consulting deck
- startup pitch template
- feature list
- crowded BP layout
- generic AI imagery
- tech-purple visual style
- corporate brand manifesto

When uncertain, choose:
- less text
- stronger atmosphere
- larger type
- darker space
- clearer shot type
- more cinematic silence
